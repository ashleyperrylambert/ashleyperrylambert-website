What’s Keeping You From Feeling Like Yourself Again?

You’re doing all the things. You’re working out, eating well, listening to podcasts, setting goals, and trying to be a good mother, wife, friend, and woman. And you still don’t feel like yourself.

Take the Quiz

You do not need another list of things to fix.

You need clarity about where to begin. Take the quiz to discover which area of your transformation needs your attention first.

In a few minutes it will point you toward one of four starting places.

Where your transformation may need to begin

Four possible starting places.

01

You are disconnected from yourself

Your life may look good from the outside. You’re capable, responsible, and trying. But somewhere along the way you lost touch with who you are underneath all of the doing.

02

Your self-trust needs rebuilding

You’ve started over enough times that you’ve stopped believing yourself when you say this time will be different. That is a repairable thing, and it starts smaller than you think.

03

Your body is asking for more support

Sometimes the answer really is physical. Strength, nutrition, recovery, and energy all affect how much of your life you are able to participate in.

04

Your life needs to make room for you

You’ve built a life around everyone else’s needs and expectations. Redefining it means deciding, on purpose, what this next season is actually going to look like.

Your body should support your life.Your life should not revolve around your body.
What happens after

The 5-Day Reinvention Reset.

Once you know where to begin, the quiz leads into a free five-day experience. It is not a fitness challenge. It introduces the whole-woman approach and helps you create movement in five areas:

  • Seeing yourself clearly
  • Renewing the stories and beliefs keeping you stuck
  • Rebuilding self-trust
  • Strengthening and supporting your body
  • Redesigning something in your everyday life
